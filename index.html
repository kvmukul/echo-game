echo

Put on headphones. You can barely see, so listen.

Find the low, steady tone: that is the exit. Something is hunting you in the dark.

Move: WASD or arrows. Ping: Space (echoes show walls and hunters, but they hear it too). Strike: F. Left or right in your ears is left or right on the map. Higher pitch means further up. Press 1 to 9 to jump to a level.

Click or press any key to start

Desktop keyboard needed. Every level builds a new maze.
